Our Story

One family, three generations, one loom.

Sonali Paithani works directly with weaving families in Paithan and Yeola — the two towns where the real, hand-thrown-shuttle Paithani is still made. Every saree on this site is stock we hold ourselves; nothing here is drop-shipped.

01

Sourced, not mass-produced

Each piece comes from a named weaving family, tracked by loom and weaver.

02

Checked before it's listed

Zari purity, border symmetry and pallu weight are checked in-shop before a saree goes online.

03

Try before you commit

Book a fitting-room slot or a video call — most brides don't buy sight-unseen, and neither do we expect you to.
