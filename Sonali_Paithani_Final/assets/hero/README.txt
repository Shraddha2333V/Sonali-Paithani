HOW TO ADD REAL HERO PHOTOS
============================

Drop 5 photos into this folder with these exact filenames, and the
homepage hero will automatically start using them instead of the
generated colour backgrounds — no code changes needed. Rotation is
every 10 seconds, looping continuously, same as it works today.

  hero-1.jpg   Bridal / friend-group mood
               A relaxed group shot — bride's side or bridesmaids in
               Paithani sarees together. Sets the "wedding season" tone.

  hero-2.jpg   Formal portrait mood
               Two people, posed, well-lit studio or heritage-building
               backdrop — showcases the weave and zari border up close.

  hero-3.jpg   Heritage / campaign mood
               A styled, editorial-looking shot — the kind of image
               that could carry a "Legacy" or "Heritage" headline.

  hero-4.jpg   Festive celebration mood
               Candid, joyful, mid-celebration (Haldi, Diwali, a
               get-together) — flowers, laughter, movement.

  hero-5.jpg   Getting-ready / warmth mood
               An intimate, close moment — helping each other get
               ready, jewellery, soft lighting.

Technical notes:
- Recommended size: at least 1600px on the longer side, landscape-ish
  or a 4:3–16:9 crop works best (the hero fills the full browser width).
- JPG or WEBP, ideally under ~400KB each after compression so the page
  stays fast — TinyPNG or Squoosh work well for this.
- Only use photography Sonali Paithani owns or has a paid licence
  for (your own shoot, a licensed stock photo, or a photographer's
  work you've paid for usage rights on). Don't reuse another shop's
  or brand's photography — that's a copyright issue even for a demo.
- If you only have 2 or 3 photos ready, that's fine — just add
  hero-1.jpg and hero-2.jpg etc.; missing files fall back to the
  generated colour background automatically, nothing breaks.
